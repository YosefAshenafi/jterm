# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone is welcome to participate in this project: filing issues, opening pull
requests, reviewing code, and joining discussions. We want that participation to
be free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and be patient with newcomers.
- Accept review feedback gracefully. Disagreement is fine; hostility is not.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances of any kind.

## Enforcement

Report violations by opening an issue, or contact the maintainer privately
through GitHub if the report is sensitive. Reports are reviewed promptly and
kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues,
and pull requests that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently
ban anyone whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
